About us

No hype, no shortcuts, no boring products.

We believe in thoughtful work and practical creativity. Every idea starts as a problem we actually had, gets tested in the wild, and only ships once it's useful, beautiful, and built for real life.

  1. 01

    Start with a real problem

    Everything we make began as a frustration of our own, like wrestling skis in an icy parking lot.

  2. 02

    Test it in the wild

    Prototypes go up mountains, into showers, and out into the rain long before they go on sale.

  3. 03

    Listen more than we talk

    Our community shapes what ships. Honest feedback beats a big launch every time.

  4. 04

    Leave it better

    Every product we make gives back to a good cause. Doing well should mean doing some good, too.

Some ideas become brands you can buy. Others stay in the lab.
