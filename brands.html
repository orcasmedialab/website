01 RainierRack

Engineered for the parking lot.

A sleek, portable magnetic rack that holds your skis or snowboard against your car. Every mountain day starts at the car, so RainierRack turns your door into a gear station: skis upright, boots on, and nothing sliding into your paint.

Feature 01

Magnetic base

Grips the door panel so the rack stays put while you gear up, then lifts right off when it's time to roll.

The rack that gives back

5% of every purchase goes to grassroots groups: volunteer trail crews, avalanche-safety education, and programs that get first-time skiers onto snow.

Unblemished paint, fresh skis

If you don't love it within 30 days, send it back for a full refund. If it ever performs less than perfectly, it's replaced free.
